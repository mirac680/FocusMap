# 🚀 FocusMap - Zeymir Edition

> A modern and sleek web assistant designed to boost your personal discipline, track daily habits, and measure your quality of life.

## ✨ Key Features

* **📊 GitHub-Style Heatmap:** Visualize your completed tasks throughout the year, plan holidays, and track your discipline.
* **🔥 Habit Streak:** Track how many days in a row you have worked without breaking the chain.
* **💎 Quality of Life & Daily Notes:** Rate each day from 1 to 10, note down the most important event of the day, and review your past archive.
* **📜 History Archive:** Easily read your past ratings and daily notes in chronological order.
* **🧠 Lessons Learned & Reminders:** List your life rules and key takeaways on the homepage and update them whenever you want.
* **🎨 Modern Color Themes:** 6 different themes to instantly switch according to your mood (Green, Turquoise, Pink, Red, Yellow, Black).
* **🌍 10 Language Support:** Full compatibility with Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, and Arabic (with RTL support).
* **💾 Data Backup (Import/Export):** Back up your data in JSON format with a single click or restore it so you never lose anything.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

This project is developed using pure web technologies without requiring any external libraries or frameworks:
* **HTML5** (Semantic structure)
* **CSS3** (Modern Flexbox, CSS Variables, Theme engine)
* **Vanilla JavaScript** (DOM manipulation, localStorage management, i18n translation system)
